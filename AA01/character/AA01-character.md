entrepreneur
/ˌäntrəprəˈno͝or/
An entrepreneur is a person who spots a market opportunity, takes the initiative, and builds a business or enterprise while assuming financial and personal risks to generate profit or create value.

What an Entrepreneur Does

• Identifies Problems: Finds gaps, inefficiencies, or unmet needs in the market.
• Gathers Resources: Combines capital, labor, ideas, and technology to bring a concept to life.
• Drives Innovation: Develops new products, services, or business models rather than just running a standard trade.
• Assumes Risk: Invests personal time, effort, and capital with no guaranteed return.

Key Characteristics

• Resilience: The ability to bounce back from failure or setbacks.
• Vision: A clear sense of direction for where the product or company can go.
• Adaptability: Willingness to pivot and adjust as market conditions shift.
• Leadership: The skill to guide and inspire a team.

Types of Entrepreneurship

• Small Business: Local shops, family businesses, and independent service providers.
• Startups: Scalable, high-growth tech or product ventures looking for outside investment.
• Corporate Entrepreneurship (Intrapreneurship): Innovating new ventures from inside an established organization.
• Social Entrepreneurship: Building ventures primarily designed to solve social or environmental issues
